Stop opening the same filtered list every Monday. Pick a model, a filter and the columns once, and Odoo emails the list as a table and an Excel file, daily, weekly or monthly.

Odoo 17.0 Community & Enterprise Free · LGPL-3

See it in action

The email your team gets: unpaid customer invoices, grouped by customer, with subtotals and a grand total.

Key features

Any list in Odoo

Invoices, deliveries, leads, tasks: pick the model, build the filter with Odoo's domain editor and choose the columns.

Table and Excel file

The list is in the email body, and an .xlsx file with every row is attached.

Groups and totals

Group by customer, salesperson or status; amounts get a subtotal per group and a grand total.

Daily, weekly or monthly

Every day, on chosen weekdays or on a day of the month, at the hour you pick in the owner's timezone.

Respects access rights

Each report reads the records as its owner, so it never mails a record the owner may not see.

Date periods

Limit the list to the last 7 days, this week or last month, on any date field. Skip the email when nothing matches.

One report: the filter, the group by and the columns, in the order they appear.

The schedule, and every sent report logged in the chatter with its Excel file.

Get started

  1. Install Simple Scheduled Reports from the Apps menu. Give the users who build reports the group Scheduled Reports Manager (the administrator has it).
  2. Open Discuss › Scheduled Reports and click New: pick a model, a filter, columns, a schedule and the recipients.
  3. Click Send Now to get the first email at once; after that it arrives on schedule.
